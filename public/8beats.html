A story of sharing

It started in school — a shared obsession with gaming soundtracks, a Facebook group, friends exchanging their favourite tracks. As the collection grew and the community widened, so did the ambition. After months of planning, navigating technical and financial obstacles together, the station officially launched in September 2021.

Connecting cultures

8Beats is a global venue for everyone who takes video game music seriously. Our goal is to build connections and celebrate the diverse creative community behind this subculture — composers, record labels, content makers, and DJs. The station runs round-the-clock thematic programming, from classic gaming audio to contemporary independent discoveries.
